# What your first vending machine really costs
Source: https://youtu.be/vGMf01krWUk | Scraped: 2026-10-15

Note: This is a 30-second YouTube Short. No spoken transcript was available (subtitles disabled) and audio analysis detected minimal speech content. The video appears to use on-screen text/visuals rather than narration.

Video description: "The machine isn't the only cost of your first vending machine. Budget for delivery, a card reader, insurance, inventory, and restocking cash before you buy."

## Key Insights
1. **Machine price is just the headline**: First-time operators consistently underestimate total startup cost by focusing only on the equipment sticker price.
2. **Hidden first-machine costs to budget for**:
   - Delivery/freight
   - Card reader/payment system
   - Insurance
   - Initial inventory
   - Restocking cash (float/working capital)
3. **Cash flow planning matters**: "Restocking cash" implies you need working capital beyond the first fill — ongoing inventory replenishment requires cash reserves.
4. **Kande takeaway**: When pitching financing or ROI calculators to prospects, explicitly surface these hidden costs. Kande's full-service model should bundle/describe these clearly so operators know their true first-machine investment. Consider creating a "True Cost of First Machine" one-pager that lists equipment + delivery + card reader + insurance + inventory + 90-day restocking reserve.
